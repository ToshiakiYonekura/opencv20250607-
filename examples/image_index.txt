VIEWING OPTIONS:
================
1. ASCII Art View: ./image_viewer_environment ascii <filename>
2. Color Analysis: ./image_viewer_environment color <filename>
3. Statistics: ./image_viewer_environment analyze <filename>
4. Feature Detection: ./image_viewer_environment features <filename>
5. HTML Gallery: ./image_viewer_environment gallery
6. Convert to PNG: convert <filename.ppm> <filename.png>
7. Online Viewer: Upload to photopea.com or convertio.co
